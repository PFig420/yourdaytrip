 Canastra do Fidalgo
 Situated in the colourful beach of Costa Nova, famous for its houses in stripes, between the sea and the Aveiro Lagoon, the restaurant Canastra do Fidalgo enjoys a beautiful location by the Costa Nova marina.
 Situated in the colourful beach of Costa Nova, famous for its houses in stripes, between the sea and the Aveiro Lagoon, the restaurant Canastra do Fidalgo enjoys a beautiful location by the Costa Nova marina.
Canastra do Fidalgo has a truly welcoming flair. It serves the most delicious sea-based specialities. Fresh fish and seafood are really the highlights of this marvellous restaurant, placed at the heart of Costa Nova!
Among the most mouth-watering specialities, it is worth highlighting: oysters, fresh fish chevice, Bulhão Pato clams, baked octopus in the oven with spices' rice, eel stew, grouper pasta with almonds and clams, among others. The cookie cake is also a true must!
